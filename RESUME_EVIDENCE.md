## Claim rules

1. Use only fictional, privacy-safe demonstrations.
2. Tie every number to a saved record, deterministic fixture, test, or versioned evaluation run.
3. State sample size beside accuracy, coverage, processing-time, or workflow-rate metrics.
4. Do not convert development scenarios into claims about real-company savings or legal outcomes.
5. Recheck this ledger before copying a bullet into a resume, portfolio, or interview presentation.
