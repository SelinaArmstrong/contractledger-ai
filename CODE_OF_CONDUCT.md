# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone who takes part in this project — filing an issue, sending a patch,
reporting a vulnerability, or commenting on someone else's — is expected to
help keep it a harassment-free experience, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and taking other people's intent charitably
- Accepting constructive feedback, and giving it about the work rather than
  the person
- Acknowledging mistakes plainly and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the people using it

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing someone's private information — a physical address, an email
  address, anything else — without their explicit permission
- Anything a reasonable person would recognise as inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit comments — and whenever someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through GitHub:
open a private security advisory (**Security → Report a vulnerability**),
which is the private channel this repository has available, and say that the
report concerns conduct rather than a vulnerability. All reports will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will
be respected.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and
other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and will give
reasons when doing so. Contributors who behave in ways this document rules out
may be temporarily or permanently barred from participating.
