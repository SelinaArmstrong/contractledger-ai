### Longer description

Contract data should be more than a collection of extracted values. ContractLedger AI shows how agreements and supplier documents can become accountable operational records: each material value connects to evidence, a reviewer and a decision history.

The workbench covers contract review, rule-driven approvals, contract and supplier registers, amendments, qualification documents, obligations and AI evaluation. AI proposes structured information; deterministic rules govern workflow requirements; people confirm material decisions.

Built by Selina Armstrong as a portfolio project for contract administration, legal operations and procurement operations. The demonstration uses fictional data. Its focus is reliable contract administration, with negotiation and e-signature outside the current scope.

## Messaging boundaries

Describe implemented capabilities and link to the demo. Do not claim unmeasured time savings, production accuracy, paying customers, compliance certification or autonomous legal decisions. Avoid “full CLM”, “enterprise ready”, “guaranteed compliance” and fabricated testimonials.

UTM examples preserve `/about` as the canonical page. No analytics collection or external publication has been enabled by this kit. The Chinese copy is promotional material; it does not imply a translated product interface or a Chinese site route.
